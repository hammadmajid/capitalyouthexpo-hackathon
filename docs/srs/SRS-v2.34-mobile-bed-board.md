# SRS v2.34 · Mobile Bed Board with rent colours

CHG-048 supplements BED-001/002, RES-004/005, RENT-004, PAY-004..008 and SERV-002. User-directed 2026-11-17.

- The mobile Rooms & rent tab (Owner and Manager) opens on a visual Bed Board: Building and Floor filters, bed totals (total, occupied, vacant, paid, due, overdue), building cards, floor bars and one room card per row on phones (two or three columns on wide screens), with a colour key. At the user's request the tab shows nothing beyond the reference: the former tiles (occupancy by date, Rent Board, payment-proof queue) and room-type cards are no longer opened from it. Room types, prices and availability stay in Hostel Setup. A user without `stay_manage` still sees the room list as before.
- Bed colours: green Paid, yellow Due, red Overdue, light blue Proof sent (a resident proof waits for review), silver Reserved (booking or hold), white Vacant; grey for blocked or off beds and for an occupied bed with nothing billed. Each bed's status is spoken, never shown by colour alone.
- Rent status per occupied bed comes only from server invoice balances (receipt-backed allocations and agreed grace). `paid` needs at least one paid invoice and no open balance; `due` means an invoice that has reached its due date, or is part paid, still has a balance (an invoice issued early is neutral until its due date, as on the Rent Board, Q5); `overdue` means past due and grace; `proof_pending` means a submitted proof waits for review and, as on the Rent Board (Q5), outranks the invoice states; it never means paid. `clear` means nothing billed yet and is never shown green (PAY-008, non-negotiable 3). It is returned only to `finance_view` holders, for today. This is management-only and is not public disclosure (BR-05 unchanged).
- Separation (p.38) is kept: the Rent Board remains its own screen for payment work. The Bed Board shows occupancy plus a single rent colour per bed.
- Tapping a vacant bed opens Add Resident with that bed chosen. Tapping any other bed opens its details in a bottom sheet (phone) or side panel (wide screen): resident, dues and rent history, payment proofs (opened by short-lived private links), transport subscription, and the existing authorised Stay actions. Bed history lists upcoming claims, the current resident and past residents in date order; each opens the original Stay.
- API (additive, no migration): occupancy rooms carry `building` and `floor`; `rentStatus` adds `paid`, `due` and `proof_pending`; one-bed occupancy adds `upcoming` claims; staff payment-proof and transport-subscription lists accept `stayId`. Every read keeps its existing branch-scope and grant checks (Owner: own hostels; Manager: assigned branches).
